About Kizuna International Education

Honest guidance and trusted institutional relationships across Japan.

Our Story

A Focused Pathway to Japan

Kizuna International Education Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted Japan-focused education consultancy based in Kathmandu, Nepal. Established in 2018 A.D. (2074 B.S.), we are officially registered with the Office of the Company Registrar and licensed by the Ministry of Social Development, Bagmati Province. We are committed to providing transparent guidance and personalized support to students pursuing higher education and language studies in Japan.

The name Kizuna (絆) means “bond” in Japanese — a principle that reflects our commitment to every student. We believe our responsibility extends beyond admissions and visa approvals. From your first consultation to your successful transition into life in Japan, we are with you at every stage of your journey.

Backed by firsthand experience of Japan and years of combined Japanese language teaching expertise, our team provides practical, reliable guidance tailored to each student’s goals. Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all approach, we help students make informed decisions that lead to long-term success.

Our services include educational counselling, Japanese language training, JLPT and JFT preparation, school and university placement, documentation assistance, visa application support, pre-departure orientation, and post-arrival guidance.

At Kizuna, we don’t just help students study in Japan. We help them build a successful future with confidence.

Mission, Vision & Values

What Guides Every Student Decision

Our Mission

To take students from their first question about Japan to their first day in a Japanese classroom — with honest counselling, real language skills, and paperwork that actually gets approved. No shortcuts, no false promises, just a clear path from Kathmandu to Japan.

Our Vision

To be the consultancy Nepali students trust before anyone else asks. We want to be known not for the number of students we send, but for how few of them run into problems once they get there.

Our Values

We only transfer visa fees after COE approval, directly to the official receiver, because trust has to be earned before it’s charged for. We answer the phone after a student lands in Japan, not just before they enroll. That’s what “Kizuna” means to us: a relationship that doesn’t end at admission.

From the Director

Director’s Message

Dear Students and Parents/Guardians,

Greetings from Kizuna International Education Pvt. Ltd.

Choosing the right educational consultancy is one of the most important decisions for students planning to study abroad. While there are many consultancies offering overseas education services in Nepal, only a few possess the in-house expertise and comprehensive knowledge of the rules and regulations related to international education, university and college admissions, financial assistance, and visa procedures. Moreover, hardly a few of them maintain high ethical standards and transparency in their operations.

At Kizuna International Education, we are committed to providing honest, reliable, and personalized guidance throughout every stage of your study abroad journey. Our experienced team offers comprehensive support, including university and college selection, admissions counselling, documentation, visa application assistance, and pre- and post-departure guidance.

Our mission is to help students achieve excellence in their academic and career aspirations by connecting them with reputable and recognized institutions. Over the years, we have successfully guided and supported many students in their international education goals. Their trust, success, and positive feedback continue to inspire us to provide even higher standards of service.

Whether you have already decided to study abroad or are still exploring your options, we warmly invite you to visit our office. We would be delighted to discuss your goals, answer your questions, and help you choose the pathway that best suits your future.

We look forward to being a part of your educational journey and helping you build a successful future.

Thank you.

Warm regards,
